Frequently Asked Questions

What does a residential interior designer do?

A residential interior designer plans how a home looks and functions. That includes layouts, finishes, lighting, furniture, window treatments and styling, along with coordination of the people who install them.

Can you help with a home that is still being built?

Yes. Involving a designer early lets us advise on tile, cabinetry, fixtures and lighting before selections become urgent, which usually leads to better results and fewer changes.

Do you work with my architect and contractor?

Absolutely. We coordinate with your builder, architect and trades so drawings, specifications and schedules stay aligned.

Can you design just one or two rooms?

Yes. Many clients start with a living room or primary suite. We scale our services to your project, whether that is one space or an entire interior.
